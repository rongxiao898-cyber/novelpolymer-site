TECHNICAL FAQ

Questions for application planning

Which product lists the lowest shrinkage range?

TZ-PLC-302 lists a shrinkage range of 2–3%.

Which products support more flexible stress accommodation?

TZ-PLC-301 is the flexible option, while TZ-PLC-303 combines flexibility with a lower listed Tg.

What should be evaluated in the finished assembly?

Evaluate the actual substrates, bond line, optical alignment, curing access and intended environmental conditions.

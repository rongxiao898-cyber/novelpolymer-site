TECHNICAL FAQ

Questions for application planning

What is the main difference between TZ-FEP-401 and TZ-FEP-402?

The published viscosity range is the main distinction: TZ-FEP-401 is lower viscosity and TZ-FEP-402 provides more controlled flow.

Is TZ-PRT-501 a permanent structural adhesive?

No. It is a temporary process-protection material with water-soluble removal.

What process details should be provided?

Provide the materials, geometry, dispensing method, available cure conditions and finished-part inspection requirements.

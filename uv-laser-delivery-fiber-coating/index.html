TECHNICAL FAQ

Questions for application planning

What should be shared for UV or deep-UV fiber evaluation?

Provide the wavelength range, fiber structure, target optical behavior, coating dimensions, process conditions and finished-fiber transmission test method.

Does a low refractive index guarantee laser-delivery performance?

No. Wavelength, glass and coating geometry, bend conditions, defects, process quality and the complete finished-fiber test all affect performance.

Does the medical-fiber application label establish certification?

No. It identifies an intended application only. Device safety, sterilization compatibility and regulatory qualification require separate evidence.

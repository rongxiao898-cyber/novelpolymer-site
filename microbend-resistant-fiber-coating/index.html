TECHNICAL FAQ

Questions for application planning

What information is needed for product selection?

Provide the fiber and cable structure, coating dimensions, expected handling loads, temperature range and microbend test method.

Why are no numerical specifications shown?

The product is available, but approved public numerical parameters are not yet listed. TIANZE can discuss the evaluation requirements directly.

Should the coating be evaluated by itself?

Finished-fiber and cable-level testing is important because microbend performance depends on the complete structure and process.

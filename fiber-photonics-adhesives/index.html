TECHNICAL FAQ

Questions for application planning

Why are separate adhesives used in one optical assembly?

Different locations can require different flow, rigidity, flexibility, cure and optical characteristics.

How should an adhesive be selected?

Define the material role, substrates, bond geometry, cure process, operating environment and finished-assembly tests.

Can a product be selected from a single data point?

No. Viscosity, cure, hardness, shrinkage, optical requirements and reliability conditions should be considered together.

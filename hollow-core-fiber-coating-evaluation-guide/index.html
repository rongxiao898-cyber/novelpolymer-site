HOLLOW-CORE FIBER GUIDE

How to Evaluate Coatings for Hollow-Core Optical Fiber

Coating selection for hollow-core and microstructured fiber should be tied to the glass geometry, coating process and the optical and mechanical behavior required from the finished fiber.

Discuss your applicationView all technical guides
01

Document the fiber structure

Record the hollow-core or microstructured glass design, outer diameter, target coating dimensions and any geometry that affects coating application or finished-fiber handling.

02

Define the role of each coating layer

State whether the material provides a compliant primary layer, external protection or another function. In a dual-layer system, evaluate the layer balance as one coating system.

03

Map the coating to the draw and cure process

Document application method, line conditions, coating concentricity, cure access or energy and dimensional controls. Confirm that the process maintains the intended fiber structure.

04

Specify handling and environmental conditions

Define bending, winding, cabling, temperature, humidity and any pressure or chemical conditions that the coated fiber will encounter.

05

Validate the finished fiber

Inspect coating integrity and dimensions, then measure the optical, mechanical and environmental behavior required for fiber produced with the intended geometry and process.

INFORMATION CHECKLIST

Prepare these details for a productive material discussion.

  • Hollow-core or microstructured glass geometry
  • Target coating layers and dimensions
  • Draw, application and cure conditions
  • Handling and environmental requirements
  • Finished-fiber inspection and acceptance tests

TECHNICAL FAQ

Questions to clarify before evaluation

Can a hollow-core fiber coating be selected from bulk material data alone?

No. The glass geometry, coating dimensions, draw and cure process and finished-fiber tests should be evaluated together.

Why should coating concentricity be included in the evaluation?

The coating is applied around a geometry-sensitive fiber, so dimensional consistency should be checked as part of finished-fiber inspection.

Which application details should be shared with the supplier?

Share the fiber structure, coating dimensions, process setup, handling environment and the optical and mechanical acceptance tests.

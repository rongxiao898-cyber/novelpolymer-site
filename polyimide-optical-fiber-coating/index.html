TECHNICAL FAQ

Questions for application planning

Does a 550°C decomposition temperature mean continuous use at 550°C?

No. It is a cured-film material-analysis value, not a continuous-service rating.

What information is needed to evaluate PI-250?

Provide the fiber design, coating dimensions, processing conditions, operating environment and required evaluation methods.

Is PI-250 the same material route as a dual-layer UV-curable system?

No. PI-250 is a polyimide coating route and should be evaluated according to its own processing requirements.

IBR PROCESS GUIDE

How to Evaluate Adhesives for Intermittently Bonded Ribbon

An IBR adhesive should be evaluated as part of the complete ribbon design because bond geometry, cure processing and finished-ribbon handling interact.

Discuss your applicationView all technical guides
01

Define the ribbon construction

Record the fiber count, coated-fiber dimensions, ribbon arrangement and the dimensional limits that must be maintained during manufacturing and handling.

02

Specify the intermittent bond pattern

Define bond position, spacing, geometry and permitted variation. The pattern and adhesive should be evaluated together rather than as independent choices.

03

Map dispensing and curing to the line process

Document application accuracy, line conditions, cure access, cure setup and inspection points needed to maintain a repeatable intermittent bond.

04

Plan rollability and fiber-access tests

Evaluate whether the finished ribbon can be handled and rolled as intended, and define how individual-fiber access will be checked without assuming performance from the adhesive alone.

05

Qualify the finished ribbon

Use application-relevant dimensional, handling, mechanical and environmental tests on ribbon produced with the intended fibers, bond pattern and process conditions.

TECHNICAL FAQ

Questions to clarify before evaluation

Should the adhesive be selected before the bond pattern is defined?

No. Bond pattern, ribbon geometry, dispensing and cure conditions should be developed and evaluated as one manufacturing system.

Is a short cure cycle enough to establish IBR suitability?

No. Cure behavior must be considered together with bond consistency, ribbon handling, rollability and access to individual fibers.

Why does the guide not recommend one universal numerical specification?

The required values depend on the ribbon design, bond pattern, line process and acceptance tests. These conditions should be defined before numerical limits are agreed.

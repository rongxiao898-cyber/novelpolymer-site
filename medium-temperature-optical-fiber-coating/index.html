TECHNICAL FAQ

Questions for application planning

Does 150–200°C mean continuous service at every temperature in that range?

No. Define the actual temperature, duration, cycling and atmosphere, then qualify the finished coated fiber for that profile.

What should be measured after thermal exposure?

Use application-relevant optical, mechanical, adhesion, crack, color and coating-integrity checks with defined baselines and recovery conditions.

Is this the same material route as PI-250?

No. 2916 / 3520 is a dual-layer coating system, while PI-250 is a polyimide coating route with different processing requirements.

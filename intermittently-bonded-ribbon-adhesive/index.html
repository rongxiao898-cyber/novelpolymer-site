IBR adhesive

Intermittently Bonded Ribbon Adhesive

An adhesive for intermittently bonded ribbon manufacturing, supporting flexible ribbon handling, rollability and access to individual fibers.

Evaluation focus

  • Controlled bond geometry
  • Rapid UV curing
  • Ribbon rollability and fiber access

TECHNICAL FAQ

Questions for application planning

Why are no numerical specifications shown?

The product is available, but approved public numerical parameters are not yet listed.

What should be defined before evaluation?

Define the fiber count, bond pattern, ribbon geometry, UV process and handling tests.

Should rollability be tested on the finished ribbon?

Yes. Bond design, process conditions and the finished ribbon structure all affect handling and fiber access.

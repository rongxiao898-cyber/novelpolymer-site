Privacy & Cookie Notice

Google Analytics is optional on this website and loads only after you choose to accept analytics.

Last updated: 6 October 2026. This notice applies to novelpolymer.cn, operated by Guangzhou Tianze New Material Technology Co., Ltd. (TIANZE).

What this website measures

If you accept analytics, Google Analytics helps us understand which pages and engineering resources are useful and how visitors reach technical-enquiry links. The measurements can include page paths and titles, general browser and device information, referring source, approximate location derived from technical connection data, scroll depth, PDF resource downloads, checklist-copy actions and clicks on enquiry or outbound links.

We do not send enquiry form fields, copied checklist text, email addresses, email content or the contents of your messages to Google Analytics. Resource events identify only the public PDF filename or that a checklist-copy button was used. Our configuration requests IP anonymization and disables Google advertising signals.

Cookies and local storage

Before consent, the website does not load the Google Analytics tag and does not create Google Analytics cookies. Your choice is saved locally in your browser under tianze_analytics_consent_v1 so the website can remember it. If you accept, Google Analytics may create cookies beginning with _ga. If you later withdraw consent, analytics is disabled and those cookies are removed where the browser permits.

Your choice and third-party processing

Analytics is optional. Choosing “Necessary only” does not restrict access to any product or technical page. You can change your choice at any time using the button below or “Cookie settings” in the site footer.

Google processes analytics data as our service provider under its own privacy and data-processing terms. See Google’s Privacy Policy for information about Google’s processing and international transfers.

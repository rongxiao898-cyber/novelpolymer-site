PHOTONICS RELIABILITY GUIDE

Photonics Adhesives: Thermal Cycling and Damp Heat

Environmental qualification should measure the finished bonded assembly, because substrates, bond geometry, cure state, optical alignment and moisture paths interact during thermal cycling and damp heat.

Send your application briefView all technical guides
01

Define the assembly and failure modes

Document substrates, surface preparation, bond-line geometry, adhesive placement, cure sequence, optical path and the alignment, adhesion, cracking, corrosion or moisture-related failures to be screened.

02

Establish a reproducible baseline

Record material batch, dispensing, fixture and complete cure conditions, then measure optical and mechanical baselines after a defined stabilization period using the same instruments planned for post-exposure checks.

03

Specify environmental sequences

Define temperature limits, ramp and dwell, cycle count, humidity, duration, powered or unpowered state and recovery conditions. Use the application requirement or agreed standard rather than an unspecified generic cycle.

04

Measure during and after exposure

Where required, monitor optical performance during exposure and repeat alignment, insertion loss, visual, mechanical and bond-integrity checks at defined intervals and after recovery.

05

Use controls and failure analysis

Include unexposed controls and enough specimens for the decision rule. Investigate where and how a failure occurred instead of attributing every change to one bulk adhesive property.

INFORMATION CHECKLIST

Prepare these details for a productive material discussion.

  • Assembly geometry, substrates and surfaces
  • Dispense, fixture and complete cure record
  • Thermal-cycle and damp-heat sequence
  • Optical, mechanical and visual measurements
  • Controls, sample count and acceptance rule

TECHNICAL FAQ

Questions to clarify before evaluation

Does a bulk adhesive Tg predict thermal-cycling reliability?

No. The substrate combination, bond geometry, cure state, moisture exposure and assembly constraints must be evaluated together.

Should optical alignment be measured only after the test?

Not necessarily. Define baseline, in-exposure if required, immediate post-exposure and recovery measurements according to the application risk.

Does completing a generic damp-heat test prove product qualification?

No. The sequence, assembly, sample count, acceptance criteria and failure analysis must match the intended application. This guide is not a qualification result.

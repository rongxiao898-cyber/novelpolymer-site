TECHNICAL FAQ

Questions for application planning

What information is needed for TZ-136 evaluation?

Provide the fiber architecture, pump wavelength, target numerical aperture, coating dimensions, draw and cure process and finished-fiber test method.

Can refractive index alone establish pump-light confinement?

No. Fiber geometry, wavelength, coating dimensions, process quality and finished-fiber measurements must be considered together.

Why should liquid and cured values be identified separately?

Refractive-index values depend on material state, wavelength, temperature and test method, so the comparison conditions must be stated.

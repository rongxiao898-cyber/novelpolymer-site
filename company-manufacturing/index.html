HOW THE PLATFORM WORKS

One application-facing team, connected capabilities.

TIANZE focuses on product selection, application development and technical communication for optical-fiber coatings and photonics bonding adhesives. Zhejiang Tsaihua supports upstream polymer design, specialty-resin synthesis, manufacturing and R&D resources.

TIANZE and Zhejiang Tsaihua are separate legal entities that collaborate across resin design, material development, manufacturing and application support.

01

TIANZE application team

Application definition, product selection, sample coordination, process discussion and customer evaluation support for fiber and photonics materials.

02

Tsaihua polymer platform

Copolymer functional-polymer and specialty-resin R&D, synthesis and manufacturing capabilities that support material development.

03

Application-to-manufacturing link

Requirements move from fiber or device structure through formulation, process evaluation, sample review and application-specific qualification.

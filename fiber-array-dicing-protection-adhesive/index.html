TECHNICAL FAQ

Questions for application planning

Is TZ-PRT-501 a permanent bonding adhesive?

No. It is a temporary process-protection material for fiber-array dicing, not a permanent structural or optical-coupling adhesive.

Does water solubility by itself define the removal result?

No. Removal time, water conditions, component geometry and the post-cleaning inspection method should be validated on the intended process.

What information should be provided for evaluation?

Provide the component geometry, protection area, dispensing and UV setup, dicing process, removal procedure and post-cleaning acceptance criteria.
